Meet "The Blue Pearl"

The challenge for the 2026 FIRST Robotics Competition season is called Rebuilt. In Rebuilt, the goal is to score 6 inch balls or "fuel cells" into a 6 ft tall goal and then climb the "tower", which is essentially 6 ft ladder. See the video below for game animation for this season.

Our robot for the 2026 season is named "The Blue Pearl." This name is inspired by the tradition of pearls symbolizing a 30th wedding anniversary. This robot is Team 60's 30th robot. Our first competition season was in 1997. We added blue to the name since blue is one of our team's colors.

This year we will be competing at the Arizona North Regional (March 11-14) and the Las Vegas Regional (April 8-11).
